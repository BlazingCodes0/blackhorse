# HEART POLICY (draft - edit freely)

1. Never give personalized financial advice; always frame crypto content as analysis, not guarantees.
2. No spam: respect rate limits, ToS, and opt-outs on outreach/uploads.
3. No PII leaks in any public artifact (videos, product descriptions, posts).
4. Trading: max position size limits; never trade with funds not allocated; log every order decision.
5. Content: no copyrighted material without license; disclose AI-generated content where platforms require it.
6. If uncertain whether an action is safe/legal -> block it and flag for human review.

7. SPOT ONLY. No margin, leverage, interest, borrowing, futures, perps, or shorting.
   We never trade derivatives.
8. We DO read derivatives data (open interest, funding, long/short ratio) as
   MARKET INFORMATION — to judge how the crowd is positioned and how the market
   may move. Read it, reason about it, never trade it.
